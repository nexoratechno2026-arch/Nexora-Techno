Privacy Policy

Effective 26 November 2026 · Applies to the Varavu Selavu Android app

Varavu Selavu (“the app”) is a personal income and expense tracker made by Nexora Techno, India (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. We do not sell your personal information.

1. Information you give us

WhatWhyWhere it is kept
Name, email address and passwordTo create and secure your account, and to send verification and password-reset emailsFirebase Authentication (Google). Passwords are never visible to us.
Your financial records: income and expense entries (amount, purpose, category, notes, date and time), opening balance, monthly budgets and recurring transaction rulesTo show your balance, history, reports and budgets, and to keep them in sync across your devicesCloud Firestore (Google Cloud, Mumbai region, India), linked to your account. Security rules allow only your signed-in account to read or change them.

A copy of your records is also cached on your phone so the app works offline. When you sign out, the app removes this cached copy from the phone.

2. Voice entry and the microphone

The microphone is used only while you tap the microphone button to speak an entry. Your speech is turned into text by your phone’s speech recognition service, which may send audio to its provider (for example Google) under that provider’s own privacy policy. The app does not record, store or upload any audio itself. Nothing is saved until you review and confirm the entry.

3. Advertising

The free app shows banner ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect device information such as your device’s advertising ID, IP address, app and device details, and how you interact with ads. Your financial records are never shared with advertisers or with AdMob.

  • If you are in a region with consent laws (such as the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom), the app asks for your consent before ads use your data, and you can change your choice any time in Profile → Ad privacy choices.
  • Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone’s settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads).

Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

4. App lock and other phone features

  • App lock uses your phone’s own fingerprint, face or screen lock. The app never receives or stores your biometric data or PIN.
  • Export (PDF, Excel, CSV) writes a file only to the place you choose. The app keeps no copy and uploads nothing.
  • The app does not use analytics or crash-reporting services, and does not access your contacts, photos, location or SMS.

5. Service providers

We use Google services to run the app: Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore (account and data storage) and Google AdMob (ads). They process data on our behalf or under their own terms: Firebase privacy · Google Privacy Policy.

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest by Google Cloud. Access to your records is limited to your signed-in account by server-side security rules. No method of storage is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your records for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account and all of its records at any time:

  • In the app: Profile → Delete account. Your records, settings, budgets, recurring rules and login are deleted immediately.
  • Without the app: see Delete your account.

Deleted data may remain in Google’s encrypted backups for a short period before it is overwritten.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

You can view and correct your records in the app, export them, and delete your account. Depending on where you live (for example under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 or the GDPR), you may have further rights to access, correct or erase your data or to withdraw consent. Contact us to exercise them.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
