Delete your Varavu Selavu account

Varavu Selavu by Nexora Techno

Option 1: in the app (immediate)

  1. Open Varavu Selavu and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Delete account.
  3. Enter your password and tap Delete permanently.

Your account and all of its data are deleted straight away.

Option 2: by email (if you no longer have the app)

Email nexoratechno2026@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Varavu Selavu account”, sent from the email address you registered with. We will confirm the request and delete your account and data within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your login (name, email address and password)
  • All income and expense records, notes and categories
  • Opening balance, monthly budgets and recurring transaction rules

Nothing is kept after deletion, except that data may remain in Google’s encrypted backups for a short period before it is overwritten. Files you exported to your own device are not affected.
